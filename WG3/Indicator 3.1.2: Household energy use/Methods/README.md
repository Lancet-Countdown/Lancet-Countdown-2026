## Methods

Access to clean energy is defined by the IEA (2020) as:

> "a household having reliable and affordable access to both clean cooking facilities and to electricity, which is enough to supply a basic bundle of energy services initially, and then an increasing level of electricity over time to reach the regional average."

Data from the **International Energy Agency (IEA)** on residential energy flows and energy access provide an indication of both access to electricity and the proportion of different types of energy used within the residential sector. These data provide an important picture of how energy access and energy use may interact.

The use of energy in the residential sector is drawn from the IEA extended global residential modelling produced in the **World Energy Outlook**, using the **World Extended Energy Balances 2025 edition**. The dataset covers all countries or major regions globally for the period **1990–2023**.

Values are measured in **EJ** and cover all fuels supplied for consumption within the **residential sector** (`IEA flow code QGFLOW076`) final energy demand.

### Energy Source Groupings

The specific IEA variables were combined as follows:

- `Solid biofuels` = Charcoal + `Primary solid biofuels`

- `Coal, coke and peat` = `Hard coal (if no detail)` + BKB + `Petroleum coke` + `Patent fuel` + `Coke oven coke` + `Brown coal (if no detail)` + Peat + `Gas coke` + `Peat products` + `Coking coal` + `Sub-bituminous coal` + `Other bituminous coal` + Lignite + Anthracite + Bitumen

- `Other biofuels` = `Other liquid biofuels` + Biogasoline + `Non-specified primary biofuels and waste` + `Biogases` + `Biodiesels`

- `Liquid fossil fuels` = `Paraffin waxes` + `Other oil products` + `Naphtha` + `Gas/diesel oil excl. biofuels` + Lubricants + `Natural gas liquids` + `Other kerosene` + `Liquefied petroleum gases (LPG)` + `Fuel oil` + `Motor gasoline excl. biofuels` + `Crude oil`

- `Waste & other` = `Municipal waste (non-renewable)` + `Municipal waste (renewable)` + `Industrial waste` + `Refinery gas` + `Blast furnace gas` + `Gas works gas` + `Coke oven gas` + `Oil shale and oil sands`

The following variables were provided directly from IEA flow `QGFLOW076` for consumption within the residential sector:

- `Natural gas`
- `Heat`
- `Solar thermal`
- `Geothermal`
- `Electricity`

## Caveats and Limitations

IEA data are generated using both direct input from national governments and modelling. As a result, the data are subject to caveats that vary by energy commodity and country.

Full details are provided in the **IEA World Energy Balances documentation, July 2025 edition**.

This documentation also describes changes to methodology introduced in previous editions of the IEA World Energy Balances.
