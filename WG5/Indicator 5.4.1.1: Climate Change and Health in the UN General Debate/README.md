## Brief description of the indicator
This indicator tracks government engagement in health and climate change in two key forums. This part of the indicator tracks mentions of climate change and health in statements made by national leaders at the United Nations General Debate, which is part of the annual United Nations General Assembly, as proxy of high-level political engagement on these two topics as separate and related issues.
