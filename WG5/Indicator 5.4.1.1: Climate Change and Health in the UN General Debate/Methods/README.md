# Methods

Indicator 5.4.1 measures the proportion of UN member states that simultaneously address **climate change and health** in their annual statements to the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) General Debate.

The time series covers **Sessions 25–80 (1970–2025)**.

For detailed methods, data sources, and analytical procedures, please see **[Methods 5.4.1.1](./Methods%205.4.1.1.docx?raw=1)**.
