## Brief description of the indicator
This indicator focuses on mapping environmental suitability for pathogenic Vibrio spp. in coastal zones globally, defined as areas within 10km of the coastline. The indicator uses thresholds of >18°C for Sea Surface Temperature (SST) and <28 PSU for Sea Surface Salinity (SSS) as suitable for Vibrio.
