## Brief description of the indicator
This indicatorestimates vulnerable populations' exposure to heatwaves by combining annual heatwave days per grid cell with gridded demographic data for older adults (≥65 years) and infants (<1 year) to calculate exposure in person-days. A heatwave is defined as at least three consecutive days where minimum and maximum temperatures exceed the 95th percentile for the area based on a 1986–2005 baseline.
