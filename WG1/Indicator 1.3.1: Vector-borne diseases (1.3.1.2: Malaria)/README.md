## Brief description of the indicator
The malaria suitability indicator tracks the historical climatic suitability for Anopheles mosquitoes, specifically in relation to the two main malaria-causing parasites: Plasmodium falciparum and Plasmodium vivax. Suitability is defined as the coincidence of several climatic factors: monthly precipitation exceeding 80mm, relative humidity greater than 60%, and average temperatures ranging from 16–32°C for P. vivax and 18–32°C for P. falciparum.
