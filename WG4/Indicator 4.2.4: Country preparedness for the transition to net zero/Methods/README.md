# Methods

The indicator builds upon the World Bank indicator of **Transition Risks – Preparedness of Countries for a Low-Carbon Transition**.

Factors contributing to resilience include:

- Macroeconomic stability and performance
- Strong institutions
- Developed financial markets
- Robust infrastructure and technological advancement
- Advanced human capital
- Access to renewable resources
- Green finance availability

Vulnerability factors include:

- A country's reliance on fossil fuels, both as a primary source of government revenue and for energy production
- The carbon intensity of key economic sectors
- The proportion of income earned from sectors highly exposed to transition risks
- The extent of income inequality

Data for each variable are sourced from the respective databases for all available years and compiled to investigate patterns of missing values.

Observations for individual variables are recovered for as many years as possible. In cases where specific years have missing values, variables are imputed at the country level if the last available observation is reasonably recent.

This process produced a cross-sectional dataset, where some countries have a complete set of observations.

For detailed methods, data sources, and limitations, please see **[Methods 4.2.4](./Methods_4.2.4.docx)**.
