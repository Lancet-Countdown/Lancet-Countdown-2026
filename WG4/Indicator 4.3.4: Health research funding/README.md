## Brief description of the indicator
Health research provides the foundation for effective public health and healthcare interventions. As climate change changes the burden of disease, it also affects how health research budgets should be prioritised. New to this year, this indicator uses data from the Dimensions database, covering 8.1 million research grants from more than 700 funders in 44 countries, to estimate the share of health research focused on climate change. Climate-and-health grants are identified using project titles, abstracts, keywords, and Dimensions research classification tags.
The focus is on research about climate change impacts on human health, rather than grants on reducing greenhouse gas emissions or mitigation co-benefits.
Health-related grants are identified using health keywords and relevant research classifications such as ANZSRC, HRCS, and UoA.
