# Methods

The data for this indicator are sourced from **IRENA** for renewable energy employment and **IBISWorld** for fossil fuel extraction employment.

Renewable industries included are:

- Hydropower
- Solar heating/cooling
- Solar photovoltaic
- Wind energy
- Bioenergy
- Other technologies

**Bioenergy** includes liquid biofuels, solid biomass, and biogas.

**Other technologies** include geothermal energy, ground-based heat pumps, concentrated solar power, municipal and industrial waste, and ocean energy.

Fossil fuel extraction values include **direct employment**, whereas renewable energy jobs include **direct and indirect employment** (e.g., equipment manufacturing), except for large hydropower, which includes direct employment only.

---

## Data

- **Employment in renewables:** IRENA and ILO, 2025, *Renewable Energy and Jobs: Annual Review 2025*, IRENA.
- **Employment in fossil fuel extraction:** *Global Coal Mining Industry Data and Analysis*, IBISWorld: oil and gas exploration and production; and coal mining, 2026.
- **Employment in fossil fuel extraction:** *Global Oil & Gas Exploration & Production Industry Data and Analysis*, IBISWorld: oil and gas exploration and production; and coal mining, 2025.

---

## Caveats and Limitations

1. Fossil fuel extraction values include **direct employment**, whereas renewable energy jobs include **direct and indirect employment** (e.g., equipment manufacturing), except for hydropower.

2. Historic IBISWorld data can change noticeably from year to year if a new analyst changes the methodology.
