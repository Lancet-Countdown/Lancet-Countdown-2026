# Methods

This indicator uses the **CDP Annual Cities Survey** to capture:

1. cities that have undertaken a climate change risk or vulnerability assessment
2. the perceived vulnerability of public health assets to climate change
3. climate change adaptation actions undertaken or currently being undertaken by cities and states

The CDP is a global non-profit organisation that runs an independent environmental disclosure system for companies, capital markets, cities, states and regions to manage and report their environmental impacts.

Using data from the **2025 CDP Annual Cities Survey**, Indicator 2.1.3 captures self-reported information from cities and states on climate risk and vulnerability assessments, climate-related health impacts and adaptation actions.

## Climate risk and vulnerability assessments

### Module 2.1

Module 2.1 of the CDP questionnaire asked cities and states:

> Has a climate risk and vulnerability assessment been undertaken for your jurisdiction? If not, please indicate why.

Eight response options were provided. These included:

- Yes, a climate risk and vulnerability assessment has been undertaken
- No, but one is currently being undertaken and will be completed within the next year
- No, but there is an intention to undertake one within the next two years

Cities and states could select only one response.

Cities or states selecting one of these three options were classified as **having completed, currently conducting, or planning to conduct within two years a climate change risk assessment**.

A total of **902 cities and states** responded to the climate change risk or vulnerability assessment module.

Of these, **874 cities and states** reported whether they had undertaken a climate change risk assessment. These 874 responses formed the baseline dataset for the risk assessment analysis.

## Climate change impacts on health

### Module 4.8

Module 4.8 asked cities and states to:

> Report on how climate change impacts health outcomes and health services in your jurisdiction.

Five response options were provided, and respondents could select multiple options.

The three main health-related areas were:

- **Public Health Outcomes**
- **Health Systems**
- **Areas outside the health sector, but relevant to the health sector**

Cities and states could also report that:

- the jurisdiction was not yet able to measure climate change-related health impacts
- they did not know

Respondents were then asked to identify:

- the climate hazards that most significantly affected the selected health area
- the health issues associated with those climate hazards

Multiple hazards and health impacts could be selected.

### Analysis of the health module

Of the **902 cities and states** responding to the risk assessment module, **861** also responded to the health module.

Of these 861 respondents, **657 cities and states** indicated that climate change was affecting either Public Health Outcomes, Health Systems, or other sectors relevant to health.

These **657 cities and states** formed the base dataset for the health analysis.

To analyse climate hazards:

- multiple hazards could be reported for each city
- duplicate reports of the same hazard within a city were removed
- only one response for each unique climate hazard per city was retained

To analyse health impacts:

- multiple health impacts could be reported for each city
- duplicate reports of the same health impact within a city were removed
- only one response for each unique health impact per city was retained

## Climate change adaptation actions

### Module 9.1

Module 9.1 asked cities and states to:

> Describe the outcomes of the most significant adaptation actions your jurisdiction is currently undertaking.

This included actions in either the planning or implementation phase.

Eleven categories of adaptation actions were provided, and respondents could select multiple options.

Cities and states identifying an adaptation action were then asked:

- which sector or sectors the action aimed to address
- whether any co-benefits had been observed

Multiple sectors and co-benefits could be selected.

A total of **874 cities and states** responded to the adaptation action module and reported **6,318 distinct climate change adaptation actions** undertaken or currently being undertaken in 2025.

These 874 cities and states and 6,318 actions formed the baseline dataset for the adaptation analysis.

The analysis also examined:

- the sectors addressed by adaptation actions
- identified co-benefits associated with these actions

# Data

The indicator uses the following data source:

- **2025 CDP Annual Cities Survey**

# Caveats and Limitations

The CDP Annual Cities Survey is **self-reported and non-compulsory**.

Responses may therefore be subjective, and participation can vary between years and across geographical regions.

Survey uptake is lower in some parts of the world, particularly the Middle East, which may affect the representativeness of the results.
