## Methods

Climate services are the provision and use of **climate data, information, and knowledge to assist decision-making**. Climate services require appropriate engagement between the recipient of the service and its provider, together with an effective access mechanism to enable timely action.

The number of **World Meteorological Organization (WMO) National Meteorological and Hydrological Services (NMHSs)** providing climate services to the health sector is calculated based on self-reported information provided by NMHSs through the **Checklist for Climate Services Implementation**.

The questionnaire is one of the main sources of information for the **WMO Country Profile database** and is open throughout the year for WMO members to update their profile information.

For each service type, country-level data were extracted from the **WMO Climate Services Dashboard** and analysed using:

- simple country counts; and
- population-weighted coverage.

Population-weighted coverage provides a measure of the population represented by countries reporting each type of climate service.

The WMO Climate Services Dashboard describes its methodology as follows:

> This methodology outlines the process for collecting and analyzing data for the Climate Services Dashboard. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of climate policy, climate services capacities, and climate services investments at national and regional levels. Data is sourced from the Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) submitted to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), the WMO Checklist for Climate Services Implementation Survey (conducted with National Meteorological and Hydrological Services, NMHSs), and the Global Observatory for Early Warning Systems (EWS) Investments developed by WMO-UNDRR using project information from Multilateral Development Banks.

## Caveats and Limitations

The current WMO data source only considers climate services provided by **National Meteorological and Hydrological Services (NMHSs)**.

It is unclear to what extent other providers are contributing climate services used by the health sector, including:

- academic institutions and research projects;
- private-sector products;
- products from other ministries; and
- regional and global products and services.

It is therefore also unclear what proportion of climate services used by the health sector is provided by NMHSs compared with these other sources.

The WMO questionnaire is open throughout the year and can be updated at any time by WMO members. Therefore, the figures reported may change during the year.

As each country may update its profile information at different points in time, snapshots of the Climate Services Dashboard do not necessarily represent progress during a specific year. Instead, they reflect the information provided up to a particular date.

The questionnaire captures information on the **provision of climate services**, but it does not comprehensively capture the source or quality of those services, and only one of the response options addresses the utility of the climate services.

The questionnaire does not capture whether climate data originate from national meteorological observations or are derived from regional or global products.

It also does not capture the potential use by the health sector of **all-sector forecasts or outlooks** that may be accessed and used for health-related decision-making.
